Vehicle Motion Controls Release Validation

Rivian Volkswagen Group Technologies · Irvine, CA

System-level integration and regression validation for vehicle motion and propulsion control software across R1, R2, and EDV programs, from feature integration through production release.

  • Own feature-level and regression validation across R1, R2, and EDV programs for development, customer OTA releases, merge releases, and supplier ECU changes.
  • Develop DVP&R plans and translate control requirements into SIL, HIL/Dyno HIL, and vehicle verification.
  • Create and triage development and customer issues, lead root-cause investigations, and verify corrective fixes.
  • Automate Python-based post-processing and maintain traceability through JAMA, JIRA, and release reports.
  • Built a standalone AI-assisted executable during Rivian’s hackathon to identify potential failure modes from supplier ECU changes.
  • Linked identified failure modes to JAMA test plans to highlight test-coverage gaps.
  • Automated JIRA assignment to relevant teams and tracked failure tickets by supplier ECU test-plan cycle.
  • Used VIN, timestamps, software versions, and test data to generate plots for Confluence reports.
  • Review DBC, LDF, ARXML, and PCAP data to validate vehicle signals, ECU interfaces, and network behavior.
  • Use CAN/CAN FD, LIN, UDS, and Automotive Ethernet evidence to investigate software and integration issues.
  • Collaborate with controls, software integration, and supplier teams; reproduce issues, verify fixes, and strengthen regression tests.
  • Vehicle motion and propulsion control software across R1, R2, and EDV.
  • Feature-level and system-level regression for OTA and merge releases.
  • Supplier ECU update validation and interface compatibility checks.
  • Release-readiness assessment supported by test results, issue status, and verified fixes.
